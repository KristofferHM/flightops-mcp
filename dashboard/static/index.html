            How to read this board

            A colour describes the weather category at that airport, never the airport's status. A red diamond is low cloud or poor visibility, not a closed airport, and a green circle is not a promise that anything is running.

            Coverage

            The airport list is worldwide: the 3,270 large and medium airports with scheduled service in OurAirports. Live traffic is not. OpenSky's receivers are dense over Europe and North America and sparse over Africa, South America and much of Asia, so an empty patch of sky means no receiver heard anything there, not that no aircraft are flying.

            What traffic costs

            Each refresh of the traffic layer costs 4 OpenSky credits, for any region. The layer refreshes no faster than its hourly ceiling allows, and stops with the reason on the map when the ceiling is reached. A triage costs up to 9 credits.
